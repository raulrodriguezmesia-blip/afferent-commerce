# Privacy Policy

Afferent Commerce processes order, payment, telemetry, and quality-assessment data to operate the Agentic Commerce settlement workflow.

## Data We Process
- Order and settlement identifiers
- Merchant and auditor contact information
- Payment and payout metadata
- Tactile telemetry and quality-assessment signals
- Audit trail and transaction references

## How Data Is Used
Data is used to assess product quality, calculate risk, generate AI recommendations, execute PayPal payouts, maintain audit trails, and provide operational dashboards.

## Data Sharing
Payment execution is performed through PayPal. No payment credentials are stored in the frontend. Backend credentials are loaded from environment variables.

## Retention
Audit and transaction records are retained for operational, compliance, and troubleshooting purposes.

## Contact
For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer through the repository owner profile.
